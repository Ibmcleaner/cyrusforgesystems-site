How it works

  1. 01 · COLLECT

    Gather the source records

    Use each contest's standings, entry selections and recorded lines for the selected week. Preserve the cutoff used in the published snapshot.

  2. 02 · VALIDATE

    Keep differences visible

    Check entries and selections, retain ties at the cutoff, and record exclusions. Westgate and Circa remain separate.

  3. 03 · COMPARE

    Show agreement with context

    Count how many selected entries chose each side. Counts describe agreement among entries, not independent people or the probability of an outcome.

How to read this board

Compare each contest independently. A count of 2 of 4 means two selected entries chose that side; it does not mean a 50% chance of winning. The denominator remains the full selected entry pool even when an early game is excluded.

Thursday selections and the early Houston game are retained as historical evidence and marked with their original exclusions. All other rows are also historical now. No combined ranking or outcome grade is inferred here.
