Method and sources

How to read this board

Selection rule: use every entry occupying the top four places in each prior-Tuesday standings file, including all ties. Collapse identical cards to one opinion, exclude Thursday selections, preserve each book's line, and average the two deduplicated book shares. A count such as 3 of 4 means three entries; it is not a win probability.
