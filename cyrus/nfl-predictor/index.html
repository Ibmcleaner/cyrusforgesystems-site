How it works

  1. 01 · DATA

    Start from the numbers

    Gather relevant historical data and contextual inputs, using only information available at the time of each forecast.

  2. 02 · CALL

    Estimate the outcome

    Produce a projection with a confidence estimate, and flag cases where the evidence is incomplete.

  3. 03 · COMPARE

    Compare and evaluate

    Compare projections with relevant benchmarks and observed outcomes to assess performance and identify areas for improvement.

    Track record

    Starts with 2026 Week 8, the first card published automatically.

    MarketTierRecordHit rateExpected
    The first results appear after Week 8 games are graded.

    Every Strong, Moderate and Slight call is logged before kickoff and graded from the official box score. The last card before kickoff counts. Voids (player did not play) are left out. Hit rate is how often the call was right. Expected is the average chance Cyrus gave those calls: when the hit rate keeps pace with it, the confidence numbers can be trusted.

    How to read this

    Chance is how often Cyrus expects the call to be right. For quarterbacks, Cyrus's projection is set beside the market line, and the call is the side Cyrus expects him to land on.
