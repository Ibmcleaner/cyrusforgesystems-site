About

Cyrus Forge Systems, LLC was founded in 2026 by Robert Legorreta, a telecommunications and enterprise systems professional with nearly 30 years of experience. For nearly nine years at Verizon, a Fortune 500 company, he supported the telephony platforms behind more than 100 contact centers and built the automations that fit their day-to-day workflows.

Robert is passionate about AI and automation, and he backs that passion with study. He holds credentials in artificial intelligence, data analytics with SQL and Tableau, Python automation, and AWS Amazon Connect, and he keeps adding to them. Cyrus Forge Systems is where that work becomes practical: the tools we build for clients, and the ones we build for ourselves, like the Cyrus NFL Predictor.

Before Verizon, he spent two decades in wireless retail and operations leadership, from Best Buy's wireless division to directing employee relations and training at Cricket Wireless. That experience shapes how we work: automation only succeeds when it fits the people who use it.
